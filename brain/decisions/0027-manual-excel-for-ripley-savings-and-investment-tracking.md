# ADR 0027: Ripley savings and the investments come in through a manual Excel

## Context

[ADR 0025](0025-savings-goal-projection-counts-liquid-savings-only.md) planned Banco Ripley as a
third bank with its own PDF parser. Banco Ripley does not let the owner download statements, so
there is nothing to parse. The owner will instead type the movements month by month into an Excel.
The owner also wants to follow how the investments held elsewhere (three Tyba funds and Flip) grow
or shrink month by month, to see their returns. ADR 0025 kept them out of the savings goal; it did
not say they could not be tracked.

## Decision

- **One manual workbook** (`~/finance-data/manual/finanzas-manual.xlsx`, outside the repository)
  with a sheet `Ahorros` (savings accounts, starting with Ripley) and a sheet `Inversiones`. A
  template with the exact columns is generated by `scripts/make_manual_templates.py`; the columns
  and rules are in [`docs/manual-data.md`](../../docs/manual-data.md).
- **Ripley savings** become statements like the PDF ones (one per account and month, opening and
  closing balance), so they reach silver and gold and count as liquid savings. A month with no
  movements is a `cierre de mes` row with amount 0 (a balance marker, not a transaction).
- **Investments are tracked separately from the savings goal**: rows are contributions,
  withdrawals and month-end valuations, and a month's return is the change in balance that the
  contributions and withdrawals do not explain. They do not enter the projection's starting balance
  or its cash flow (ADR 0025 stands).
- **The real Excel is treated like a real PDF**: it never enters Git, and its structure is shared
  only as a masked dump the owner reviews first.

## Alternatives considered

- **Treat investments as one more bank statement**: they have no statement and no opening/closing
  pair; what matters is the return net of the owner's own contributions, which a statement does not
  express.
- **Count the investments in the savings goal after all**: rejected earlier (ADR 0025); tracking
  their return does not change that.
- **Ask for a parser of the Ripley app's export**: there is none to ask for.

## Consequences

- The importer (workbook to statements, plus the investment return calculation) is a task of its
  own, designed from the masked dump of the owner's real file.
- With about three months of history and few movements, the first returns are short series: they
  say little until more months accumulate.
- Reconciliation is only as strong as the balances the owner types: a row's `saldo_final` lets the
  importer check that the movements add up, and a mistyped one will be reported, not absorbed.
- Fees and taxes are not modelled yet (a `comision` column would be added if a fund charges them).
